# Telecommunications ChurnQuest – Customer Churn Analysis Using Excel

## Project Overview

The Telecommunications ChurnQuest project focuses on analyzing customer data to identify patterns, trends, and factors associated with customer churn. Using Microsoft Excel, various analytical techniques were applied to understand customer behavior, service usage, account characteristics, customer support interactions, and revenue indicators. The goal was to generate actionable insights that can help reduce customer attrition and improve customer retention.

## Objective

* Identify key factors influencing customer churn.
* Understand customer behavior and service usage patterns.
* Analyze customer retention and revenue impact.
* Evaluate customer support interactions and their relationship with churn.
* Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer satisfaction and business performance.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* COUNTIF Function
* AVERAGE Function
* Correlation Analysis
* Scatter Plots
* Data Visualization Techniques (Bar Charts, Pie Charts, Trend Analysis)

## Process

1. Performed data validation and checked for missing values.
2. Formatted and prepared the dataset for analysis.
3. Conducted exploratory data analysis on customer behavior, service usage, revenue, and churn.
4. Performed statistical analysis including churn rate calculations, trend analysis, and correlation analysis.
5. Created visualizations using Pivot Charts, Bar Charts, Pie Charts, and Scatter Plots.
6. Generated business insights and developed strategic recommendations for customer retention.

## Key Insights

* Customer churn is influenced by multiple factors including service quality, customer experience, usage behavior, and account characteristics.
* Customers with frequent customer service interactions tend to have higher churn rates.
* Higher customer engagement is associated with stronger retention and lower churn.
* Retaining high-value customers is essential for protecting business revenue.
* Long-term customers generally provide greater lifetime value and business stability.

## Business Recommendations

* Improve customer support response time and issue resolution processes.
* Introduce loyalty programs and rewards for long-term customers.
* Implement targeted retention strategies for high-value customers.
* Regularly optimize service plans to meet evolving customer needs.
